## 1. Deep Audit of Nested Validation & Selection Optimism

### What Was Verified
1. **Zero Positional Assumption**: All three OOF prediction files (`oof_catboost.csv`, `oof_tuned_lightgbm.csv`, `oof_xgboost.csv`) were merged strictly on `SK_ID_CURR`.
2. **Probability Validity**: All 171,202 predictions per model are strictly bounded in $[0.0, 1.0]$ with zero NaN, infinite, or missing values.
3. **Outer Fold Exclusion from Weight Selection**: In the Stage G1 nested routine, for each outer fold $k$, the candidate blend weights were evaluated solely on samples from the other 4 folds ($S_{-k}$), and the selected weight configuration was tested on fold $k$ ($S_k$).
4. **Target & Identifier Isolation**: `TARGET` and `SK_ID_CURR` were never used as predictors in any model.

### Technical Nuance: Why Fixed OOF Blending Carries Mild Selection Optimism
While the individual predictions in each OOF file are out-of-sample relative to the model that predicted them, using fixed OOF files in a post-hoc nested procedure is **not completely equivalent to a fully nested cross-validation**:
1. **Component Model Overlap**:
   - In Stage G1, the predictions for inner folds $S_{-k}$ were generated by models $M_j$ ($j \ne k$).
   - However, every model $M_j$ was trained on a training fold that **included** outer fold $S_k$'s data!
   - Thus, the inner predictions used to choose weights were produced by models that had already seen the outer evaluation samples during their primary training.
2. **Prior Hyperparameter Selection**:
   - The hyperparameters for LightGBM (Config 2: `num_leaves: 24`, `max_depth: 7`, `min_child_samples: 100`), CatBoost (`depth: 6`, `l2_leaf_reg: 5.0`), and XGBoost (`max_depth: 6`, `subsample: 0.8`) were chosen by evaluating the 5-fold cross-validation performance across all 171,202 training samples.
   - Therefore, the component models themselves were already optimized on the full dataset before the blending weights were evaluated.

### True Fully Nested Pipeline (Ideal Benchmark)
To be mathematically unbiased, a full nested CV would require:
- An outer loop of 5 folds.
- Within each outer fold (80% data), an inner 4-fold CV is executed from scratch to tune hyperparameters, generate inner OOF predictions, and select blend weights.
- Only then are outer-fold test samples evaluated.

### Practical Impact on Our Results
Our Stage G1 measured difference between the overall OOF score (`0.76187`) and the post-hoc nested score (`0.76184`) was **only +0.00003**. Because the candidate set consisted of only 13 fixed, pre-defined heuristic weights, the degree of selection optimism is practically negligible. However, we do **not** claim the `0.76184` score is completely free from hyperparameter selection bias.

## 4. Final-Training & Inference Architecture Plan

To generate test predictions for `test.csv` (61,500 rows), we have evaluated two potential strategies:

### Strategy A: 5-Fold Test-Time Bagging (Recommended / Standard Competition Practice)
Rather than retraining a single model on 100% of data without validation feedback, we leverage the 5 fold models already validated during cross-validation:
1. For each of the 5 folds:
   - Fit `CatBoost`, `LightGBM`, and `XGBoost` on the training fold with early stopping on the validation fold.
   - Transform `test.csv` using that fold's fitted `BaselineFeaturePipeline`.
   - Generate test probabilities from each model.
2. Average test predictions across the 5 folds for each architecture:
   $$\hat{P}_{\text{CB}}(\text{test}) = \frac{1}{5} \sum_{k=1}^5 \hat{P}_{\text{CB}, k}(\text{test})$$
   $$\hat{P}_{\text{LGB}}(\text{test}) = \frac{1}{5} \sum_{k=1}^5 \hat{P}_{\text{LGB}, k}(\text{test})$$
   $$\hat{P}_{\text{XGB}}(\text{test}) = \frac{1}{5} \sum_{k=1}^5 \hat{P}_{\text{XGB}, k}(\text{test})$$
3. Compute the final ensemble probability using the locked weights:
   $$\hat{P}_{\text{final}} = 0.50 \cdot \hat{P}_{\text{CB}}(\text{test}) + 0.25 \cdot \hat{P}_{\text{LGB}}(\text{test}) + 0.25 \cdot \hat{P}_{\text{XGB}}(\text{test})$$
4. **Why Strategy A is Superior**:
   - Eliminates guesswork on number of training iterations for 100% data.
   - Provides an additional variance-reduction bagging benefit across the 5 models.
   - Directly mirrors the cross-validation setup.

### Strategy B: Full 100% Single-Model Refitting (Alternative)
- Fit 1 CatBoost, 1 LightGBM, and 1 XGBoost on all 171,202 training samples.
- Fix boosting iterations to the median optimal rounds observed in CV (CatBoost: ~900, LightGBM: ~250, XGBoost: ~480).
- Predict once on `test.csv` and blend 50% / 25% / 25%.

*Recommendation: We strongly recommend Strategy A (5-Fold Bagging) for maximum test set generalization and stability.*
